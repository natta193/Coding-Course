TAKE-HOME PROJECT: Finish Higher or Lower
After lesson P07 (Project – Higher or Lower) - about 1–2 hours, in chunks over the next few days.

Finish the milestones you didn't get to in the session, then make the game your own.

Milestones (make each one work before starting the next):
  [ ] 1. Finish milestones 2–5 (difficulty, lives, play again, best score)
  [ ] 2. Add the reverse mode where the computer guesses YOUR number
  [ ] 3. Make the computer as smart as possible – how many guesses does it need at most for
         1–100? Why?
  [ ] 4. Ask someone at home to play and watch them – fix anything that confuses them

Stretch: Add a two-player mode: player 1 types a secret number (hide it by printing 50
blank lines), player 2 guesses.

Bring it to the next session - working or stuck, both are fine.

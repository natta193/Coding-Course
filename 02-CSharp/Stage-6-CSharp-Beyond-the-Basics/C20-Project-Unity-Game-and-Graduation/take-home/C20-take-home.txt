After lesson C20 (Project – Unity Game and Graduation) - about 2 hours per gap (4–6 gaps), in chunks over the next few days.

Your graduation game. One milestone per gap; every session starts with a playtest of the
latest build.

Milestones (make each one work before starting the next):
  [ ] 1. Gap 1: grey-box prototype (M1)
  [ ] 2. Gap 2: core mechanic + win/lose (M2)
  [ ] 3. Gap 3: UI, menus, restart (M3)
  [ ] 4. Gap 4: content – levels or waves (M4)
  [ ] 5. Gap 5: polish – art, sound, particles (M5)
  [ ] 6. Gap 6: build it, playtest with 3 people, fix, rehearse your graduation demo

Stretch: Publish a WebGL build on itch.io (with a parent's OK) and send the link to
family.

Bring it to the next session - working or stuck, both are fine.
